01

Echelon Right / Echelon Left

Echelon Right:

  • 1: leader
  • 2: to the leader's right, slightly behind (30-45 degrees)
  • 3: to the right of 2, also behind
  • 4: to the right of 3, behind

Echelon Left: same, but toward the left.

The formation reads as a diagonal line from the leader toward the selected side, with every following aircraft stepped back from the aircraft ahead.

1234
02

Line Abreast

  • 1: leader in the center or slightly ahead
  • 2: to the leader's left, same distance
  • 3: to the leader's right
  • 4: farther left or right depending on the variant

The flight forms a horizontal line: 1 stays central or slightly forward, 2 and 3 sit on either side, and 4 extends the selected side.

2134
03

Trail

  • 1: leader
  • 2: directly behind the leader
  • 3: behind 2
  • 4: behind 3

The aircraft occupy one straight longitudinal line, with each aircraft directly behind the previous one.

1234
04

Staggered Trail

  • 1: leader
  • 2: behind the leader, offset to the right
  • 3: behind 2, offset to the left
  • 4: behind 3, offset to the right

The line remains longitudinal, but the aircraft alternate from right to left as they move behind the leader.

1234
05

Vic / V-Formation

  • 1: leader at the point
  • 2: behind and to the left
  • 3: behind and to the right
  • 4: behind the leader or reinforcing one wing

The leader is the point of the V, 2 and 3 form the two rear wings, and 4 closes the center or reinforces one side.

1234
06

Diamond

  • 1: leader at the point
  • 2: behind and to the left
  • 3: behind and to the right
  • 4: directly behind the leader, closing the diamond

The four positions close into a diamond: the leader at the front, two aircraft across the rear shoulders, and 4 directly behind.

1234
07

Combat Spread

  • 1: leader
  • 2: to the leader's right, 1-2 km separation
  • 3: behind the leader, slightly offset
  • 4: behind 2, slightly offset

The formation opens laterally, with 2 and 1 separated across the front and 3 and 4 offset behind their respective forward positions.

2134
08

Fluid Four

  • 1: leader
  • 2: leader's wingman (right or left)
  • 3: lead of the second element, behind and lateral
  • 4: wingman of 3

The first pair is 1 and 2; the second pair is 3 and 4, positioned behind and laterally from the first pair.

1234
09

Arrowhead (helicopters)

  • 1: leader
  • 2: behind and to the left
  • 3: behind and to the right
  • 4: farther back, centered

The helicopter group forms an arrowhead: 1 at the point, 2 and 3 behind on the wings, and 4 centered farther back.

1234
10

Box

  • 1: front left
  • 2: front right
  • 3: rear left
  • 4: rear right

The aircraft occupy the four corners of a box, with 1 and 2 forward and 3 and 4 directly behind them.

11

Pincer

  • 1: leader in the center
  • 2: left flank
  • 3: right flank
  • 4: support behind the leader

The leader stays in the center while 2 and 3 hold the two flanks and 4 remains behind as support.

2134
12

Bracketing

  • 1: left of the target
  • 2: right of the target
  • 3: support behind 1
  • 4: support behind 2

The target is bracketed between 1 on the left and 2 on the right, with 3 behind 1 and 4 behind 2.

13

Crossfire (helicopters)

  • 1: left of the target
  • 2: right of the target
  • 3: behind 1
  • 4: behind 2

The helicopter positions place the target between the two forward aircraft, while 3 and 4 remain behind their respective sides.

14

Pop-Up Attack

  • 1: leader performs pop-up
  • 2: behind the leader, providing cover
  • 3: left lateral
  • 4: right lateral

1 leads above the formation during the pop-up, 2 stays behind for cover, and 3 and 4 occupy the two lateral positions.
