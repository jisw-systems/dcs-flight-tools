Mi-24P Hind

Cold Start

Taxi & Takeoff (Essential)

Landing (Essential)

Technical Notes

  • Preserve speed: regain control of the engagement without stalling.
  • Break turn: maximum-performance turn with 30° or more of bank; normally 180°. Counter the descent with collective and use flares if needed.
  • Combat turn: climbing U-turn, up to 30° nose-up and 45° of bank. Exit above 100 km/h.
  • Zoom climb: start above 200 km/h and do not exceed 30° nose-up. Recover between 150 and 100 km/h by pushing the cyclic forward.
  • Climbing turn: recover from the zoom with 30° of bank between 150 and 100 km/h; apply slight collective to maintain level flight on exit.
  • Dive: do not exceed -30° or 350 km/h. If retreating-blade stall occurs, lower the collective and pull back on the cyclic.
